Voice (everything runs in your browser)

Like a walkie-talkie: hold, speak, let go. Start with a pet's name to talk to that pet (“Pip, where is the charger?”). Pets and the Teacher each have their own voice, and you only hear those near you; a pet answering you from far away comes over the radio.

          Save the simulation

          A file holds the whole simulation (world, pets, teacher, history, thoughts). Keep it, move it to another machine (say, from the hosted site to your own), and load it there. Loading a file adds it to the list below, then asks before it replaces the live world.

          Saved simulations

          Loading replaces the live world, pets, teacher and history, and leaves the simulation paused. A backup of what you had is kept automatically (the latest 3).

            Pet brain library

            Save a pet's brain from the Pets tab. A brain carries personality, beliefs, what it was told, its goal and recent memories, and can be put into any pet here or in another simulation.
